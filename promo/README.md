# ShopKeeper: promo brief

Reference pack for a horizontal promotional hero image inside the ShopBack app.
The five screenshots are straight from the current build (portrait, 900 × 1600).
The hero image itself is not in this folder; it is to be made from this brief.

## The game in one line

**Entrepreneur by Day. Adventurer by Night. You are the Shop Keeper!**

ShopKeeper is a single-player mobile game that lives inside the ShopBack app.
By night you fight through a neon Techno Dungeon for loot. By day you sell that
loot in your own shophouse, haggle with customers, and switch on a ShopBack
campaign to pull in a crowd.

## Core mechanics

| Mechanic | What the player does | Why it matters for the image |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Run the shop** | Walk the shop floor, choose what each display case shows, set prices. | Warm wood, red carpet, string lights: the cosy half of the game. |
| **Haggle** | One thumb on a green-to-red slider: take the offer, counter, or hold firm. Each counter costs the customer a patience heart. | The slider and the big price number are the most recognisable piece of UI. |
| **ShopBack campaign** | Tap the ShopBack button once a day for three times the customers. | The crowd outside the door is the payoff shot. |
| **Cashback** | Every campaign sale gives the customer Cashback. They leave delighted and some return the next day. | Always shown as magic: sparkles, pink and gold, happy faces. Never as a cost. |
| **Dungeon combat** | Virtual stick, melee combo, dodge roll, auto attack and a drag-to-aim bow. Five intro levels, then a new Daily Dungeon every day with rare loot. | Dark teal and magenta: the adventurous half, a strong contrast to the shop. |
| **Companion** | Choose and name a lucky cat or a black-and-tan shiba. It follows you, fights, carries loot and can run loot home. | The emotional hook. Both pets should appear. |
| **Promotion calendar** | Each day of the week boosts certain loot (Gear Up Monday, S-Day Saturday…). | Optional detail; a reason to come back daily. |
| **Upgrade tree** | Spend coins on the store, combat, backpack and pet. | Not needed in the hero image. |

## Hero image guidance

- **Format:** horizontal. The exact ShopBack banner size was not available when
  this was written; confirm it before production. Until then, compose for about
  2:1 and keep everything important in the middle 60% so it survives cropping.
- **Idea:** split the frame down the middle. Left is the warm shop by day, right
  is the neon dungeon by night, and the shopkeeper stands on the seam with one
  foot in each. This is the hero line as a picture.
- **Cast:** one shopkeeper (or both), the lucky cat on the shop side and the
  shiba on the dungeon side. A delighted customer or two on the shop side; a
  drone and a glitch slime on the dungeon side.
- **Props:** loot on display (gear, power cell, glowing core, gold chip, prism
  shard), coins, and Cashback sparkles in pink and gold drifting from shop to
  customer.
- **Colour:** shop side uses warm wood, cream and carpet red; dungeon side uses
  deep teal with magenta and cyan lights. ShopBack red (about `#f1492c`, taken
  from screenshots, so check against the official brand kit) for the title
  and call to action only.
- **Type:** title "ShopKeeper" plus the hero line, or the short form
  "Entrepreneur by Day. Adventurer by Night." Rounded, friendly display type
  (the game uses Fredoka).
- **Style:** soft low-poly 3D renders with chunky, faceted, toy-like characters:
  shopkeepers, customers, pets, enemies and loot all share this look.
- **Avoid:** on-screen controls and HUD, any wording that makes Cashback sound
  like a fee or a loss, small text, and real brand logos other than ShopBack's.
